# Day as My Anxiety

**Date of Experiment:** 23 May 2026  
**Person Borrowed:** My Own Anxiety (Personified)

### What I Did:
- Woke up terrified
- Overthought every single message I sent
- Assumed everyone was mad at me
- Replayed conversations from 2019
- Panicked about the future for 14 hours straight

### What I Learned:

My anxiety is exhausting. It never rests. It is paranoid, dramatic, and deeply self-centered.

Living as my anxiety for one day made me realize how much energy I waste being afraid of things that haven't happened yet.

### Final Reflection:

I have been possessed by a dramatic theater kid with an anxiety disorder for the last 8 years.

Today I fired him.

He cried. I didn't care.

— Bobby (Finally Free)
