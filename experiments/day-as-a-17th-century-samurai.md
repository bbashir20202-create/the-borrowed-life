# Day as a 17th Century Samurai

**Date of Experiment:** 24 May 2026  
**Person Borrowed:** Miyamoto Musashi (Legendary Samurai)

### What I Did:
- Woke up at 4 AM
- Trained with a wooden sword for 2 hours
- Spoke only when necessary
- Ate simply and with full attention
- Treated every small task as a matter of life and death
- Did not complain once

### What I Learned:

Modern life feels ridiculous after living one day like this.

We complain about everything. Samurai complained about nothing.

The level of discipline and presence was almost inhuman. I felt more alive in 8 hours than I have in the last 3 years.

### Final Reflection:

I now understand why the samurai were feared.

It wasn't the sword.

It was the complete lack of mental noise.

I want to become more like him.
