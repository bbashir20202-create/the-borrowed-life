# Day as My Father at Age 27

**Date of Experiment:** 26 May 2026  
**Person Borrowed:** My father when he was 27 years old

### What I Did:
- Woke up at 5:30 AM (he used to do this)
- Drank black coffee with no sugar (he still does this)
- Worked 14 hours straight without complaining
- Spoke very little
- Did not check my phone once during work
- Came home exhausted but still asked my mother how her day was

### What I Learned:

My father at 27 was running on duty, not passion.

He was carrying the weight of an entire family while barely being an adult himself. The silence I felt all day wasn’t calmness — it was emotional exhaustion.

I understood in one day why he sometimes seemed emotionally unavailable when I was growing up. He wasn’t cold. He was just tired.

### Most Surprising Realization:

I complain way more than he ever did.

I have so much more freedom and opportunity than he had at my age, yet I act like life is harder.

This experiment broke me a little.

I think I owe my father a long apology.

— Bobby
