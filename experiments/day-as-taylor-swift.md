# Day as Taylor Swift

**Date of Experiment:** 26 May 2026  
**Person Borrowed:** Taylor Swift (Midnights Era)

### What I Did:
- Wrote two songs before breakfast
- Overthought everything I said in every conversation
- Changed my outfit 4 times before leaving the house
- Spoke in clever metaphors even when ordering food
- Cried while listening to my own playlist
- Drafted 11 unsent letters to people who hurt me in 2014

### What I Learned:

Taylor Swift doesn't just feel emotions — she **collects** them like rare Pokémon and then weaponizes them into art.

The level of self-awareness mixed with self-mythology is actually genius.

Most surprising: I felt extremely lonely even though I was "famous" in my own imagination. Turns out being understood by millions doesn't fix feeling misunderstood by the few who matter.

### Final Reflection:

I now understand why she writes so many songs about exes.

It's not pettiness.

It's archaeology.
