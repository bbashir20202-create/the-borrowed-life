# Methodology - How to Borrow a Life

### Rules:
- You must commit to **one full 24-hour day**.
- You must try to think, speak, eat, and make decisions like the person.
- You are allowed to adapt to modern reality (you don’t have to ride a horse if you’re borrowing a samurai).
- Write the report within 48 hours after the experiment ends while the experience is still fresh.

### Good Subjects:
- Historical figures
- Family members (especially parents at your current age)
- Your future self
- People you dislike (this one is usually the most educational)
- Celebrities
- Fictional characters

### What to Document:
- How it felt to think like them
- What surprised you
- What was difficult
- What you learned about them
- What you learned about yourself

The goal is not accuracy.
The goal is **empathy through embodiment**.
